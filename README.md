## The entry

**abbreviation**

The word for making things short is itself twelve letters long. The joke
is the word itself: its length contradicts its meaning the moment you read
it, no explanation needed. Delete it and there is no word left for
shortening words, so every shortened word would need its full name spelled
out instead. A real word every reader recognises, spelled correctly, and a
fresher pick than the obvious "moist" or "literally" the rest of the field
reaches for first.

## How it works

`main.py` writes exactly that one word — 12 characters, no trailing
newline — to `/output/entry.txt`, inside the 1–77 character gate.
